Frequently Asked Questions

Does Pomello require an account?

No. Pomello works entirely on your device — no account, no sign-in, no internet connection required.

Where is my data stored?

All your tasks, plans, and timer settings are stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.

Will my data sync across devices?

Pomello stores data locally, so data does not sync between devices at this time.

The timer keeps running when I close the app — is that normal?

Yes. When you leave the app mid-session, Pomello uses the system time to calculate how much time has passed. When you return, the countdown will be accurate. A notification alerts you when the phase ends.

Why isn't the Live Activity showing on my lock screen?

Make sure Live Activities are enabled for Pomello in Settings → Pomello → Live Activities. The Live Activity appears automatically when you start a timer session.
